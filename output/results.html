Bird diversity and community varies with proximity to trails in urban and peri-urban green spaces

Chloe A. Cull, Mackenzie Guest, Isabella C. Richmond, Ana Morales, Carly D. Ziter, Barbara Frei

Main Takeaways

  • SR & Shannon higher in informal sites

  • SR & Shannon higher further from trails
